## 2. 两个官方约数：时间、分母、口径

原文（PRE 14C 叙述）：

> As of October 1, 2026, Mr. Zuckerberg beneficially owned 1,220,703 shares of Class A common stock and 341,215,148 shares of Class B common stock, collectively representing approximately 60.6% of the Company’s total outstanding voting power and approximately 13.4% of its total outstanding economic interests.

分母原文（同一文件，Applicable percentage ownership）：

> Applicable percentage ownership is based on 2,214,455,318 shares of Class A common stock and 341,786,026 shares of Class B common stock outstanding as of October 1, 2026.

官方持股表同一截至日：A 类 1,220,703，标注 *；B 类 341,215,148，标注 99.8；投票权列 60.6。
脚注：`* Less than 1%.`

口径要点：
- 截至日是 2026-10-01，不是股权登记日 2026-10-20。
- 13.4%、60.6%、99.8 是公司披露的约数或表内一位小数，不是作者另算后的「官方百分比」。
- 受益所有（beneficially owned），含通过信托和相关主体持有的股份。
- 5,000,000,000 / 4,141,000,000 是授权股本，不是已发行，不上图。

## 3. 作者验算（双向核对）

经济权益（股数合计 / 已发行 A+B）：

    (1,220,703 + 341,215,148) / (2,214,455,318 + 341,786,026)
    = 342,435,851 / 2,556,241,344
    = 13.39606887…%

与官方「approximately 13.4%」在一位小数上一致。

投票权（票数加权）：

    分子：1×1,220,703 + 10×341,215,148 = 3,413,372,183
    分母：1×2,214,455,318 + 10×341,786,026 = 5,632,315,578
    = 60.60335461…%

与官方「approximately 60.6%」在一位小数上一致。

B 类占已发行 B 类：

    341,215,148 / 341,786,026 = 99.83297210…%

与官方表 99.8 在一位小数上一致。

A 类占已发行 A 类：

    1,220,703 / 2,214,455,318 = 0.05512430…%

与官方 *（不足 1%）一致。不上图写成「0.1%」或「1%」。

若剔除 Biohub 登记的全部 A 类后再除以同一经济分母（作者计算，不是官方口径）：

    341,215,148 / 2,556,241,344 = 13.3484…%

会落到约 13.3%。本篇图上不另标这一替代比例，以免和官方 13.4% 抢读；只在内页说明：公司所称经济权益按受益所有口径，包含这些他并无金钱利益的 A 类股份。

## 4. 不可混用的股权登记日数据

Record Date = close of business on October 20, 2026。
当日已发行：A 类 2,214,472,367；B 类 341,768,886。
同意股东合计持有 315,826,951 股 B 类，约占当时已发行股本投票权的 56.1%，且全部由扎克伯格受益所有。

56.1% 是书面同意那一组股东在登记日的投票权，不是 10 月 1 日的 60.6%。图上不用 56.1%，也不用登记日已发行股数去重算 13.4%/60.6%。

## 6. 脚注 2：Biohub 与金钱利益

> Consists of (i) 1,220,703 shares of Class A common stock held of record by Chan Zuckerberg Biohub, Inc. ("CZ Biohub"); (ii) 3,388,097 shares of Class B common stock held of record by Mark Zuckerberg, Trustee of The Mark Zuckerberg Trust dated July 7, 2006 ("2006 Trust"); …  
> Mr. Zuckerberg is deemed to have sole voting and investment power over the shares held by CZ Biohub, but has no pecuniary interest in these shares.

更正（v3.1）：图上不得写成「全部 A 类由 Biohub 登记持有」，以免读成公司全部已发行 A 类。原文是他受益所有的 1,220,703 股 A 类由 CZ Biohub 登记持有，他被认定为有单独投票权和投资权，但对这些股份没有金钱利益。


其余 B 类通过 2006 信托及多家 CZI / Chan Zuckerberg LLC 持有。图上可简写「受益所有、经相关主体持有」，不把 13.4% 写成「他口袋里的钱」。部分 CZI I 股份有质押，本篇不展开未在图上使用的质押细节。

## 7. C 类：已批准，尚未生效

8-K（2026-10-19 董事会，2026-10-20 股东书面同意，2026-10-21 提交）：

> On October 19, 2026, the board of directors … approved and declared advisable … the amendment and restatement of the Company’s certificate of incorporation … establishing a new class of non-voting Class C common stock … which was subsequently approved by the requisite shareholders by written consent on October 20, 2026. The Company expects to file the Restated Charter … with an effective date of January 19, 2027.

PRE 14C 同样写明章程生效日 2027-01-19，并说明一次性按 1:1 派发 C 类，以章程生效为条件。

分红后 C 类持股（官方约数，按股权登记日持有情况，不是已经到手的股份）：

> After giving effect to the Recapitalization and the Dividend, based on his ownership and the total shares of Class A and Class B common stock outstanding as of the Record Date, Mr. Zuckerberg will own approximately 342,435,851 shares of Class C common stock, representing approximately 13.4% of the shares of Class C common stock to be issued in the Dividend.

该约数在数值上等于 2026-10-01 受益所有 A+B 合计 342,435,851，但官方明确按 Record Date 口径来说。图上如使用，必须标「官方约数、按股权登记日、以章程生效为条件」，不写成「就是 10 月 1 日两列相加」。

创始人对齐协议（Founder Alignment Agreement）原文：

> Pursuant to the Founder Alignment Agreement, 20% of the shares of Class C common stock to be issued to the Holder Group in the Dividend will be freely saleable and the other 80% of the shares of Class C common stock will be subject to sale and transfer restrictions（Stapled Class C Shares）. The Holder Group generally may only sell Stapled Class C Shares if, for each such Stapled Class C Share sold, the Holder Group … sell … Class A … or Class B … or convert Class B …

20%、80% 是官方对分红所得 C 类的安排，不是对现有 A/B 持股的分割。

未采用：2016 年曾撤回的 C 类方案；截至 2026-11-19，未见官方 8-K 确认媒体所称「C 类发行暂停」，故不写成事实。

## 8. 官方对控制权的表述，以及本篇边界

> Mr. Zuckerberg currently has the ability to elect all of our directors and to determine the outcome of most matters submitted for a vote of our shareholders. This will not change following the Recapitalization and Dividend.

另有「substantially all matters」的表述。本篇采用「多数事项 / 提交股东表决的多数事项」，不夸大成「一切事务」或「无限权力」。同时写明：须遵守法律和董事义务；C 类尚未生效；捆绑出售限制；控制权通过信托和相关主体持有。
