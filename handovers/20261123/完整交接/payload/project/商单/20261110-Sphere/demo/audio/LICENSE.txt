Voice
  demo/audio/vo.wav is assembled from Microsoft Edge neural TTS
  (zh-CN-YunxiNeural). Most lines use rate +12%. Shot 20 uses +35% so
  the two clauses fit the existing 2.8s graphic (and its 1.30s subtitle
  cut). Leading/trailing silence is trimmed; shot 1 is given a very
  slight atempo (~1.044) so 2.82s of speech sits inside the 2.8s hook.
  This replaced the earlier 剪映 AI 配音 plan after Fisher said not to
  use 剪映 and to pick another scheme. The 云希 voice was the one
  already preferred in the demo/audio/voice_test/ listen.
